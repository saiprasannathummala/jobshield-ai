# JobShield AI - Project Plan

## Goal

JobShield AI will analyze job postings and identify potentially fraudulent or suspicious job opportunities.

## Core Features

- Analyze job descriptions
- Detect suspicious patterns
- Generate a risk score
- Classify jobs as Safe, Suspicious, or High Risk
- Explain detected red flags
